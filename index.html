Messages From Our Leaders

Read inspiring words and visionary guidance from our honorable advisors and leadership board.

Md. Tawhidur Rahman

President, GSTU Science Club

"A science club is not supposed to be all about contesting and doing activities. GSTU Science Club endeavors to provide a place where students can engage in asking meaningful questions, testing theories, learning from their mistakes, collaborating with others, and applying knowledge to solve problems. This is our objective—to promote scientific thought processes and innovations through learning."

S M Al-Amin Nishan

General Secretary, GSTU Science Club

"I believe science has the power to transform society and build a sustainable future. My vision is to promote scientific curiosity, innovation, and research among students through impactful projects, conferences, and collaborative activities. Together, we strive to create a vibrant scientific community that addresses environmental challenges and contributes to the betterment of society."

Why Join Us?

Got questions? We've got answers. Here is what you need to know about becoming a part of our community.

❓ Why Clubbing is Essential?

Builds vital soft skills and expands your network.

❓ Why Join the Science Club?

Bridges academic theory with real-world project application.

❓ How is our club different from others?

Delivers high-impact professional output, securing grants and hosting national festivals.

❓ How will you benefit?

Builds a high-value CV and provides mentorship for scholarships and jobs.

❓ Is clubbing harmful or helpful to academics, and how do members manage time?

Helpful; keeps you productively occupied while prioritizing academics during exams.
